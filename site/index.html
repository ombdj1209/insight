A nudge with a Join button

Insight syncs your Google or Outlook calendar. Before each call you get a reminder with Join, or Join + notes to start listening in the same click. For meetings with people, a brief arrives first: what you discussed last time and what you still owe them.

14:12 · Priya asks about the payment flow

Every voice, on your PC

Insight records your microphone and whatever your PC plays, so Teams, Zoom and Meet in a browser all work and nobody sees a bot. Speakers are told apart by voiceprint, and the transcript appears as people talk.

15:01 · "What was our churn rate last quarter?"

The answer, before anyone asks twice

When someone asks something your files can answer, Live Assistance shows the answer and its exact source next to the transcript: 2.4%, from Q3 metrics.xlsx. When you hang up, Enhance turns your rough notes and the transcript into clean notes with action items.

Friday · the QA plan is due

Promises, kept

Insight pulls out who promised what, using the speaker labels, so "I'll send Sofia the QA plan" lands on your list and Daniel's vendor quote lands on his. Before a promise is due, Pip reminds you and checks your sent mail for proof.

Ask anything you've heard or saved.

Point Insight at your folders and it reads them on your PC. Ask across files and every past meeting, and each answer cites the file it came from. Connect Google or Microsoft 365 and Ask can also read your mail, calendar and Drive.

One more thing.
Meet Pip.

A small pet that lives on top of your other windows. It tells you when meetings start, takes notes when you ask, and keeps you company in between. Pick a species, a colour and a hat. It levels up as you work together.

  • Rub itHearts. Throw it and it bounces off the edge of your screen.
  • Feed it a fileDrop a PDF or a folder on Pip and Insight learns it for Ask.
  • Talk to itCtrl Shift K asks by voice, from any app.
  • It notices callsWhen Zoom, Teams, Meet or Webex takes the mic, Pip offers to take notes.
  • It knows when to hideGone during full-screen slides and videos. Ctrl Shift P hides it any time.

Runs on your PC.
Stays on your PC.

With local AI and no accounts connected, Insight makes no network calls after setup. Cloud AI is an option you turn on, and each meeting shows a badge when it was used.

What runs where
Notes, Ask and Live AssistanceGemma 4, through llama.cpp on your GPU (CUDA or Vulkan) or CPUOn your PC
Search across filesEmbeddingGemma, always local, even when notes use the cloudOn your PC
Speech to textSenseVoice and Parakeet through sherpa-onnx, or whisper.cppOn your PC
Who said whatVoiceprints, kept per meetingOn your PC
API keys and sign-insEncrypted with Windows, never shown to the app's pagesOn your PC
Cloud AIGemini or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, if you pick itYour choice
Google and Microsoft 365Read-only by default. Drafts, tasks and events only with "Allow actions" on, each one confirmed. Insight never sends mail.Your choice

Team pets.

Your pet walks over to a teammate's screen to wave, find a time that fits both calendars, hand over meeting notes or ask for the daily stand-up. It works through a folder your team already syncs, like OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox, with no new server.
