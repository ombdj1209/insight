One more thing

A pet that lives on your desktop.

It sits on top of your windows, tells you when meetings start, takes notes when you ask and levels up as you work together. Clicks pass through everywhere except the pet. Pick one, and the pet on this page changes too.

  • Rub itHearts. Drag it and throw it; it bounces off your screen edge.
  • Feed it a fileDrop a PDF or a folder on it and Ask can answer from it.
  • Talk to itCtrl Shift K asks by voice, from any app.
  • It notices callsZoom, Teams, Meet or Webex takes the mic, and it offers to take notes.
  • It knows when to hideGone during full-screen slides and games. Ctrl Shift P any time.
  • It growsXP from meetings, notes, petting and feeding. Levels unlock hats and badges.
